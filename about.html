About Us

Xander's Food Fusion opened its doors after our three founding cooks realized their family recipes had more in common than they first thought: food is a language of passion, passion that is as shareable as an appetizer. The best ways to connect are done every day. Our three locations are located near the hometowns of our three founding chefs.

We don't chase trends for their own sake. Every dish on our menu earns its place because it tastes right, not because it photographs well. Our dishes are inspired by the heritage of our chefs, each with their own signature dishes from their hometown as well as their family's home country. Our staff are trained to know the story behind every plate they serve, so don't be shy about asking your server where a dish came from — chances are it has a good one.
