Welcome to Fusion Fare

Xander's Food Fusion started as a spinoff of the popular Gracie's Twenty First Century Cafe located in Pottstown Pennsylvania. Xander's love for food soon sprawled into its own restaurant.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick lunch or settling in for a long dinner with friends, our menu changes with the seasons but our promise stays the same: bold flavor, honest ingredients, and a table that always has room for one more.

A Kitchen Without Borders

Our cooks trained on three continents before landing in the same kitchen, and it shows in every dish that leaves the pass.

Expect a menu that treats "fusion" as a verb, not a gimmick, where techniques and spices move freely between cuisines.

Sourced Daily

We build the day's specials around whatever the local market and our fishmonger deliver that morning, not the other way around.

That means the menu shifts with the seasons, but the standard for freshness never does.

Made for Sharing

Most of our plates are sized for the middle of the table, because the best part of a meal is usually the argument over the last bite.

Come hungry, bring friends, and don't be afraid to order one more thing than you think you need.
