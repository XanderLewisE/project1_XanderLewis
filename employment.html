Job Opportunities

Line Cook

We're looking for a line cook with at least a year of kitchen experience who can keep pace during a dinner rush without losing attention to detail. You'll rotate between our sauté and grill stations and work directly with our sous chef on seasonal menu changes. Prior experience with global or fusion cuisine is a plus but not required — we'll teach you our style.

Front-of-House Server

Our servers do more than take orders; they're expected to know the story and ingredients behind every dish well enough to guide a hesitant guest toward something new. We're looking for someone personable, quick on their feet, and comfortable working evenings and weekends. Restaurant experience is preferred but a great attitude matters more.

Pastry Assistant

Join our small pastry team preparing desserts and daily bread for all three dining rooms. This early-morning role suits someone detail-oriented and comfortable following precise recipes while still bringing their own ideas to seasonal specials. Culinary school background helpful but not required.
